SerenitySearch

A Modern Tool for Timeless Principles.

An AI-powered, privacy-first companion app designed to bring the wisdom of A.A. literature into the modern era. Instantly search texts, process inventory, and generate discussion topics.

Deep Literature Search

Targeted searches across 10 approved texts (Big Book, 12&12, Daily Reflections, etc.). Custom multi-select filtering guarantees you find the exact insight and daily application you need.

Interactive 12 Steps

Read the unabridged text of all 12 Steps natively. Tapping a step automatically triggers an intelligent, cross-literature search focused entirely on that step's core principle.

10th Step Spot Check

Use native voice dictation to speak what's disturbing your peace. The AI "Sponsor" processes your input to provide compassionate validation, a perspective turnaround, and the next right thing.

Meeting Topic Generator

Instantly generate fresh, context-aware meeting topics complete with a core quote, a deep reflection, and open-ended discussion questions. Seamlessly share directly from your device.

Personal Saved Library

Bookmark your favorite passages, specific 10th step turnarounds, and engaging meeting topics. Build a tailored archive of recovery insights that you can quickly reference anytime.

Deep Theme Selection

Personalize the visual experience. Toggle Dark Mode or choose from 15 meticulously crafted color palettes—ensuring maximum readability and visual comfort for any user, in any lighting.

Technical Architecture

100% Private

Zero backend tracking. Data stays entirely on your local device.

PWA Ready

Installs natively as a standalone app on iOS, Android, and tablets.

Accessible

Built-in Text-to-Speech reads passages aloud for auditory learners.

Cross-Device Sync

Secure JSON data export allows seamless transitions across devices.
